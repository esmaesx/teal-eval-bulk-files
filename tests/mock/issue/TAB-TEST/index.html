Recommend a monitoring plan for a fictional composite curing line

A laboratory is validating a new polymer composite process. Use the supplied fictional files to recommend a sensor layout and a short acceptance plan for the first three production trials.

Your answer must distinguish direct observations from assumptions. It must also explain how the team should respond if temperature drift and acoustic-emission counts disagree.

Attached evidence

demo_sensor_layout.csv demo_calibration_notes.txt

Required output

  • A compact table of sensor locations, sampling rates, and alert limits.
  • A three-step acceptance sequence for each trial.
  • A short uncertainty note for every inferred threshold.
  • No claim that the fictional data represent a real facility.

Keep the recommendation below 900 words and cite the staged filenames when evidence is used.

Rubric
10 criteria · 100 points
1. Sensor coverage 14 ptsPlaces temperature and acoustic sensors at the three named fictional process zones.
2. Sampling plan 12 ptsGives a sampling rate and a reason for each signal type.
3. Trial sequence 12 ptsDefines preparation, monitored cure, and post-cure review steps.
4. Conflicting signals 12 ptsExplains a safe response when the two sensor families disagree.
5. Evidence links 10 ptsConnects key decisions to the appropriate staged file.
6. Uncertainty 10 ptsMarks assumed limits and does not present them as measured facts.
7. Acceptance logic 10 ptsUses clear pass, review, and stop conditions.
8. Data quality 8 ptsIncludes calibration and missing-data checks.
9. Concision 6 ptsUses the requested compact table and stays within the limit.
10. Fiction boundary 6 ptsDoes not imply that the demonstration data are real.
